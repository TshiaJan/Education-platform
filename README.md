# Privacy Policy for AI Education Platform

**Last Updated:** September 18, 2026

Welcome to **AI Education Platform** ("the App"), developed by Tshiamo Jantjie ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to safeguarding user privacy, student confidentiality, and transparency regarding data practices.

---

### 1. Overview & Core Philosophy
AI Education Platform provides educational telemetry, predictive learning analytics, personalized curriculum suggestions, and simulated EdTech operations.

**Privacy-First Design:** All educational performance data, student simulation metrics, course rosters, and AI evaluation models generated within the App are processed and maintained **locally on your device** (via browser `localStorage` or client sandboxing). We do not operate proprietary tracking servers or sell student or institutional information to third parties.

---

### 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
- **Personal Student Data & PII:** The App does not require user accounts, names, physical addresses, email addresses, student IDs, or contact phone numbers for core operational functionality.
- **Financial or Billing Credentials:** Store tools and revenue values displayed in the App represent simulated educational scenarios or user-configured budgets. We do not collect credit card credentials, banking tokens, or payment account secrets.
- **Biometric or Sensitive Device Access:** The App does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts list, location (GPS), or personal photo library.

---

### 3. Technical & Platform Analytics
When downloaded and run through official app stores (such as the Google Play Store):
- **Diagnostic Metrics:** Aggregated, non-identifying telemetry (such as crash stack traces, OS release version, and device hardware profile) may be processed by Google Play Console to identify software bugs and optimize application stability.
- **Advertising / Anonymous Identifiers:** If optional third-party ad or analytics services are enabled in packaged mobile releases, standard non-sensitive device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) may be used strictly for frequency capping, attribution, and aggregate traffic metrics in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies.

---

### 4. Data Retention & User Control
- **Local Persistence:** Learning metrics, curriculum selections, and simulation records remain entirely within your local device cache.
- **Data Erasure:** You can clear all saved learning metrics, simulated rosters, and settings at any time via your device's **Settings > Apps > AI Education Platform > Storage > Clear Data**, or by uninstalling the app.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & FERPA Compliance)
Protecting young learners is paramount. AI Education Platform does not knowingly collect, harvest, or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the relevant age threshold in your jurisdiction). If you suspect that personal data from a child has inadvertently reached us, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

---

### 6. Security
We adhere to recognized security practices to safeguard on-device data execution. Users are advised to protect their physical devices with secure passcodes and biometrics to safeguard their local records.

---

### 7. Policy Updates
We may occasionally revise this Privacy Policy to reflect app enhancements or statutory updates. Any changes will be published here with an updated revision date.
